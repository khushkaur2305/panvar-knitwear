Since 1990

A knitting unit in Ludhiana, run the same way for 36 years.

Established in the year of 1990, we “K.K Knitwear Club” are a Manufacturer of Polyester Fabric, Knitted Fabric, Sportswear Fabric, Terry Fabric, Baby Blanket Fabric or Jacket Fabrics, Dot Knit Fabrics, Polyester Knitted Fabric and Dotted Fabric.

K.K Knitwear Club is a leading supplier specializing in high-quality knitted fabrics — Knitted Polyester Fabric, Dot Knit, Nirmal Knit, Sarina Fabric and Plain Micro Polyester — perfect for fashion, sportswear and casual apparel.

Order flow

From enquiry to dispatch.

Six steps, each stating only what we publish — sampling where it is offered, in-house manufacture, published MOQs, the packing formats listed on products, accepted payment modes and road dispatch.

01

Share the requirement

Construction, GSM, width, shade and quantity. The catalogue lists all four against every line, so an enquiry can be precise from the start.

02

Sample where offered

Sample orders are accepted on selected lines — rice knit, rim zim dotted knits and the bright and chandani burka fabrics among them.

03

Knitted in our own unit

K.K Knitwear Club is registered as a manufacturer with a factory premises, so gauge, GSM and width are set on our own machines.

04

Quantities from 100 kg

Most lines open at 100 kg or 100 metres; heavier programmes run at 150–200 kg or metres. Surplus stock is negotiable in bulk.

05

Packed to format

Rolls (including 20 kg and 25 kg roll packing), thans, packets or boxes, depending on the line.

06

Payment and dispatch

Cash, cheque, pay order, bank transfer or online payment. Shipment by road, banked with HDFC Bank.
