About

A knitwear house in India’s knitwear capital.

Panwar Knitwear is a knitwear manufacturing business based in Ludhiana, Punjab. The company was founded by Mohar Singh Panwar and is run today by co-founders and CEOs Prabhu Panwar and Bhala Ram Panwar, with Rohitash Panwar leading the company’s online presence.

Production runs on modern knitting and finishing machinery, and the company works with eco-friendly materials as part of its commitment to sustainable manufacturing. Every style begins with a defined fabric — from 100% cotton and dry fit for summer topwear through to spun fleece, Russian fleece and sherpa for winter wear.

Inside the unit

From yarn to packed garment

Every stage is handled in-house, so quality is controlled from the first knit through to the final fold.

  1. 01

    Knitting

    Yarn is knitted into the required fabric quality — cotton, fleece, lycra or bonded knits.

  2. 02

    Dyeing & processing

    Greige fabric is dyed to shade and processed for handfeel, shrinkage and finish.

  3. 03

    Cutting

    Fabric is laid and cut to pattern, then sized and bundled for the stitching lines.

  4. 04

    Stitching

    Panels are assembled into finished garments — hoods, ribs, zips, pockets and hems.

  5. 05

    Printing

    Chest prints and brand artwork are applied to the styles that call for them.

  6. 06

    Finishing & packing

    Garments are checked, pressed, labelled and packed ready for dispatch.

Materials

Eight knitted qualities

Spun Fleece
Brushed winter knit used across sweatshirts and hoodies.
Dry Fit
Moisture-managing knit for sports and active t-shirts.
Honeycomb Lycra
Textured stretch knit with a structured surface.
100% Cotton
Breathable natural knit for everyday topwear.
Cotton Lycra
Cotton blended with lycra for shape retention and stretch.
NS Bonded
Bonded construction giving extra warmth and body.
Russian Fleece
Dense fleece quality built for cold-weather garments.
Sherpa
High-pile winter fabric used for heavy outerwear styles.
