About us

Made Fresh, Made Local.

The Daily Crumb is a neighborhood bakery and cafe focused on freshly prepared breads, pastries, cakes and beverages. Whether you are picking up breakfast, meeting a friend for coffee, or ordering a cake for a special occasion, we aim to keep every visit simple, fresh and enjoyable.

You will find us in Main Market, where we bake through the day and are happy to take orders in advance for anything you need for a celebration at home.
