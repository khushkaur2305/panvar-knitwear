About us

Who We Are

Panwar Knitwear is a knitwear manufacturing business based in Ludhiana, Punjab — India's knitwear hub. The company was founded by Mohar Singh Panwar and is run today by co-founders and CEOs Prabhu Panwar and Bhala Ram Panwar, with Rohitash Panwar leading the company's online presence.

Our Craftsmanship

Production runs on modern knitting and finishing machinery, and the company works with eco-friendly materials as part of its commitment to sustainable manufacturing. Every style is built from a defined fabric — from 100% cotton and dry fit for summer topwear through to spun fleece, Russian fleece and sherpa for winter wear.

  • Topwear and bottomwear produced under one roof
  • Two established in-house brands: ZONIXA and MSP SPORTS
  • Eight knitted fabric qualities across summer and winter ranges
  • Bulk and custom manufacturing for brands and wholesalers

Inside the unit

Manufacturing Process

Yarn arrives at one end and packed garments leave at the other. Each stage is handled in-house so quality is controlled from the first knit to the final fold.

  1. 01

    Knitting

    Yarn is knitted into the required fabric quality — cotton, fleece, lycra or bonded knits.

  2. 02

    Dyeing & Processing

    Greige fabric is dyed to shade and processed for handfeel, shrinkage and finish.

  3. 03

    Cutting

    Fabric is laid and cut to pattern, sized and bundled ready for the stitching lines.

  4. 04

    Stitching

    Panels are assembled into finished garments — hoods, ribs, zips, pockets and hems.

  5. 05

    Printing

    Chest prints and brand artwork are applied to styles that call for them.

  6. 06

    Finishing & Packing

    Garments are checked, pressed, labelled and packed for dispatch to buyers.
